### **GLOSSARY INDEX** 

The following rules and game terms are fully defined and expanded upon in this book. This glossary is provided to give you a definition at a glance and make your life easier as a player or Director. The page numbers after each glossary entry tell you where you can read more about the subject. 

Whenever you see a rule or game term in the glossary index <u>underlined in cross-reference style, that topic has its own entry in this</u> section. Terms in the glossary index aren’t necessarily cross-referenced every time they appear, but only when it’s important to know when one rule or mechanic ties directly to other parts of the game. 

**Ability:** Special <u>main actions, maneuvers, and more that a creature can</u> use to affect other <u>creatures and objects, and the environment. p. 69</u> 

**Ability Roll:** A <u>power roll</u> made as part of using an <u>ability. Skills can’t be</u> applied to ability rolls. p. 73 

**Adjacent:** Within 1 <u>square. p. 76</u> 

**Advance Move Action:** A <u>move action</u> that allows a creature to move a number of <u>squares</u> up to their <u>speed. This movement can be broken up</u> by a creature’s <u>maneuver</u> and <u>main action. p. 272</u> 

**Agility:** A <u>characteristic that represents a creature’s coordination and</u> nimbleness. p. 3 

**Aid Attack Maneuver:** A <u>maneuver</u> that allows a creature to choose an enemy adjacent to them. The next <u>ability roll an ally makes against</u> that enemy before the start of the aiding creature’s next <u>turn gains an edge. p. 273</u> 

**Ally:** A creature who is willingly friendly to another creature. p. 73 

**Ancestry:** A <u>humanoid</u> creature’s species. Every <u>hero has an ancestry.</u> p. 21 

**Area of Effect:** The <u>squares</u> affected by an <u>ability that creates an aura, burst, cube, line, or wall. (An ability that creates an area of effect affects</u> targets simply by those targets being in its area, as opposed to a <u>strike.)</u> p. 71 

**Argument:** A plea the heroes make during a <u>negotiation to convince</u> one or more <u>NPCs to work with them. p. 287</u> 

**Artifact:** A powerful <u>treasure</u> that can unbalance the game. p. 336 

**Artisan:** A <u>follower</u> who undertakes <u>crafing projects for a hero. p. 350</u> 

**Aura:** When an <u>ability or other effect creates an aura, that area is</u> expressed as “X aura.” The number X is the radius of the aura, which always originates from the creature or object who created it, extends from the outside of the creator’s <u>space, and moves with them. p. 71</u> 

**Background:** A <u>hero’s culture and career. p. 53</u> 

**Bane:** A situational disadvantage that gives a creature a −2 penalty to a <u>power roll. p. 5</u> 

**Bleeding:** A <u>condition</u> that causes a creature to take 1d6 + <u>level</u> damage whenever they use a <u>main action</u> or <u>triggered actions, or make a power roll</u> using <u>Might or Agility. p. 77</u> 

**Bonus:** A positive number that increases a creature’s statistics or the roll of a die. p. 5 

**Breakthrough:** A <u>natural 19 or 20</u> on a <u>project roll. When a character</u> experiences a breakthrough, they can make another project roll for the same <u>project as part of the same respite activity. p. 293</u> 

**Burrow:** A <u>movement</u> mode available to creatures with “burrow” in their speed entry, or who gain the capability to temporarily burrow. Such creatures can move through dirt horizontally at full speed. p. 268 

**Burst:** When an <u>ability or other effect creates a burst, that area is</u> expressed as “X burst.” The number X is the radius of the burst, which always originates from the creature or object who created it, extends from the outside of the creator’s <u>space, and lasts only for as long as it</u> takes to affect its targets. p. 71 

**Capital:** The largest city in <u>Orden, filled with art, culture, and intrigue.</u> p. 11 

**Career:** The job a <u>hero had before becoming a hero. p. 58</u> 

**Catch Breath Maneuver:** A <u>maneuver</u> that allows a <u>hero to spend a Recovery and regain Stamina</u> equal to their <u>recovery value. p. 273</u> 

**Censor:** A <u>class for a hero who is a trained warrior devoted to a saint or god. p. 78</u> 

**Characteristics:** Statistics used to represent a creature’s mental and physical prowess, broken out as <u>Might, Agility, Reason, Intuition,</u> and <u>Presence. Each characteristic has a score that ranges from −5 to +5.</u> p. 3 

**Charge Main Action:** A <u>main action</u> that allows a creature to move up to their <u>speed in a straight line, then make a melee free strike</u> or use an <u>ability with the Charge keyword against a target</u> when they end their move. p. 274 

**Ceiling:** Any solid surface above a creature. p. 76 

**Clarity:** The <u>talent’s Heroic Resource. Unlike other Heroic Resources,</u> clarity can go below 0, leaving a talent <u>strained. p. 187</u> 

**Class:** A <u>hero’s</u> current role, which largely determines how they interact with the game’s rules. p. 69 

**Claw Dirt:** An <u>ability that uses a maneuver</u> to allow a creature without “burrow” in their speed entry to burrow. p. 268 

**Climb:** A <u>movement</u> mode that allows a creature to climb without using additional <u>squares</u> of movement. A creature without “climb” in their speed entry or the temporary ability to climb must use 2 squares of movement to climb 1 square. p. 269 

**Combat Round:** A segment of a combat encounter in which each creature participating in the battle takes a <u>turn. p. 265</u> 

**Complication:** A dramatic narrative twist that deepens a <u>hero’s</u> backstory and gives them a rules benefit and drawback. Complications are an optional rule. p. 233 

**Concealment:** A state where a <u>target</u> has their form entirely covered in a concealing effect that doesn’t block line of efect, such as darkness or fog. While a target has concealment, <u>strikes used against them take a bane. p. 276</u> 

**Condition:** A negative effect that applies to a <u>creature and uses a uni-</u> versal shorthand name. <u>Bleeding, dazed, frightened, grabbed, prone, restrained, slowed, taunted, and weakened</u> are conditions in _Draw Steel_ . p. 77 

**Conduit:** A <u>class for a hero who is the devoted spellcasting priest of a saint or god. p. 94</u> 

**Consequence:** An impactful setback suffered by a creature when they make a <u>test. A consequence can occur whether or not the creature</u> making the test succeeds or fails. p. 250 

**x** 

**Consumable:** A <u>treasure</u> that can be used a limited number of times before it is expended. p. 314 

**Cover:** A state where a <u>target</u> has at least half their form, but not all their form, blocked by a solid obstruction. While a target has cover, damage-dealing <u>abilities used against them take a bane. p. 276</u> 

**Crafting Project:** A <u>downtime project</u> undertaken to create a <u>treasure, a</u> vehicle, or some other object. p. 295 

**Crawl:** A <u>movement</u> mode that allows a <u>prone creature to move.</u> A prone creature must use 2 <u>squares</u> of movement to crawl 1 square. p. 269 

**Creature:** Living and unliving beings, including constructs and undead. p. 6 

**Critical Hit:** When a creature rolls a <u>natural 19 or 20</u> on an <u>ability roll</u> made as part of a <u>main action, that creature gains an additional main action</u> that they can use immediately. An ability roll made as part of a <u>maneuver</u> can’t score a critical hit. p. 75 

**Cube:** When an <u>ability or other effect creates a cube, that area is</u> expressed as “X cube.” The number X is the length of each of the area’s sides. A cube effect might last only as long as it takes to affect its targets, or it might have a duration specified by the effect. p. 71 

**Culture:** The community in which a <u>hero was raised. p. 53</u> 

**d3:** A three-sided die, often rolled using a d6. p. 4 

**d6:** A six-sided die. p. 4 

**d10:** A ten-sided die. p. 4 

**d100:** A hundred-sided die, usually rolled using two <u>d10s. p. 4</u> 

**Damage:** A harmful effect that reduces the <u>Stamina</u> of a creature or object. p. 277 

**Damage Immunity:** A trait that allows a <u>target</u> to reduce damage they take of a specific <u>damage type. Damage immunity is expressed as</u> “[damage type] immunity X,” or “damage immunity X” to represent immunity to all damage. Damage of the specified type dealt to the target is reduced by X. p. 277 

**Damage Type:** A classification often given to elemental and <u>supernatural</u> damage sources. Acid, cold, corruption, fire, holy, lightning, poison, psychic, and sonic are damage types in _Draw Steel_ . p. 277 

**Damage Weakness:** A trait that makes a <u>target</u> increase damage they take of a specific <u>damage type. Damage weakness is expressed as</u> “[damage type] weakness X,” or “damage weakness X” to represent weakness to all damage. Damage of the specified type dealt to the target is increased by X. p. 277 

**Damaging Terrain:** An area of obstacles that deal damage to creatures who are in the area or move through it. A creature can’t <u>shif into or out</u> of damaging terrain, and can’t <u>jump</u> out of damaging terrain. p. 270 

**Dazed:** A <u>condition</u> that limits a creature to doing only one thing on their <u>turn: use a maneuver, use a main action, or use a move action. A</u> dazed creature also can’t use <u>triggered actions, free triggered actions, or free maneuvers. p. 77</u> 

**Defend Main Action:** A <u>main action</u> that allows a creature to impose a <u>double bane on all actions made against them until the start of</u> their next <u>turn. Additionally, the creature has a double edge</u> on <u>tests</u> when called for to resist environmental effects or a creature’s traits or <u>abilities. p. 274</u> 

**Devil:** An <u>ancestry from the Seven Cities of Hell. p. 26</u> 

**Difficult Terrain:** An area of obstacles that are difficult to move through. It costs 1 additional <u>square of movement to enter a square of difficult</u> terrain. A creature can’t shift into or out of difficult terrain, and can’t jump out of difficult terrain. p. 270 

**Dig Maneuver:** A <u>maneuver</u> that allows a creature with “burrow” in their speed entry or the temporary ability to burrow to move a number of squares equal to their size vertically through dirt. p. 268 

**Director:** The player who prepares, presents, and adjudicates the game for all the other players, who each create and run a <u>hero. p. 3</u> 

###### **Discipline:** The <u>null’s Heroic Resource. p. 147</u> 

**Disengage Move Action:** A <u>move action</u> that allows a creature to shift 1 <u>square. p. 272</u> 

**Distance:** The number of squares away that a creature using an <u>ability</u> can affect targets with that ability. The “Distance” entry in an area ability also includes the type of <u>area of efect</u> created by that ability. p. 71 

**Double Bane:** When a creature has two or more <u>banes and no edges</u> applied to a <u>power roll</u> , they have a double bane on the roll. A double bane applies no penalty to a power roll, but instead automatically decreases the <u>tier outcome of the roll by one tier. p. 5</u> 

**Double Edge:** When a creature has two or more <u>edges</u> and no <u>banes</u> applied to a <u>power roll, they have a double edge on the roll. A double</u> edge adds no bonus to a power roll, but instead automatically increases the <u>tier outcome of the roll by one tier. p. 5</u> 

**Downtime Project:** A task a <u>hero undertakes during one or more respite. p. 293</u> 

**Dragon Knight:** An <u>ancestry</u> with a draconic heritage. p. 28 

###### **Drama:** The <u>troubadour’s Heroic Resource. p. 203</u> 

**Dwarf:** An <u>ancestry with stone skin and short stature. p. 30</u> 

**Dying:** A state a <u>hero enters when their Stamina</u> is 0 or lower but doesn’t reach the negative of their <u>winded value. While dying, a hero</u> is <u>bleeding</u> and they can’t use the <u>Catch Breath maneuver in combat.</u> A hero dies when their Stamina equals the negative of their winded value. p. 278 

**Echelon:** A grouping of heroic <u>levels</u> that informs players of the types of heroic deeds the <u>heroes can achieve. There are four echelons of play:</u> 1st echelon (1st to 3rd level), 2nd echelon (4th to 6th level), 3rd echelon (7th to 9th level), and 4th echelon (10th level). p. 8 

**Edge:** A situational advantage that grants a creature a +2 bonus to a <u>power roll. p. 5</u> 

**Elementalist:** A <u>class for a hero mage who wields the elemental forces</u> of the timescape—earth, green, fire, the void, and more. p. 112 

**EoT:** An abbreviation used in an ability <u>tier outcome for an effect that</u> lasts until the end of the affected creature’s next <u>turn. p. 76</u> 

**Enemy:** A creature who is hostile to another creature. p. 73 

**Enhancement:** A property given to an armor, implement, or weapon <u>treasure</u> that a hero creates as part of a <u>crafing project.</u> p. 296 

**Escape Grab Maneuver:** A <u>maneuver</u> that allows a <u>grabbed creature to</u> make an <u>ability roll to escape. p. 273</u> 

**Essence:** The <u>elementalist’s Heroic Resource. p. 113</u> 

**Experience (XP):** A hero’s <u>Victories</u> convert to Experience when they finish a <u>respite. Experience permanently increases a hero’s capabilities</u> by allowing them to increase in <u>level. p. 7</u> 

**xi** 

**Falling:** When a creature falls 2 or more <u>squares, they take 2 damage for</u> each square they fall (to a maximum of 50 damage) and land prone. A falling creature can reduce the effective height of their fall by a number of squares equal to their <u>Agility</u> score (minimum 0). p. 269 

**Ferocity:** The <u>fury’s Heroic Resource. p. 131</u> 

**Flanking:** When two or more allied creatures are <u>adjacent</u> to and on opposite sides of an enemy, those creatures are flanking that enemy. A creature flanking an enemy gains an <u>edge</u> on <u>melee strikes against that</u> enemy. p. 276 

**Fly:** A <u>movement</u> mode available to creatures with “fly” in their speed entry, or who gain the capability to temporarily fly. Such creatures can move through the air horizontally or diagonally at full speed and remain in midair. If a flying creature is made <u>prone or has their speed</u> reduced to 0, they fall. p. 269 

**Focus:** The <u>tactician’s Heroic Resource. p. 175</u> 

**Forced Movement:** When an ability or effect compels a creature to move, usually against their will. There are three types of forced movement: a pull, a push, and a slide. Forced movement is always along the <u>ground unless noted as vertical. Forced movement can be reduced by stability. p. 270</u> 

**Follower:** An <u>NPC</u> dedicated to helping a <u>hero. Many of the actions of a</u> follower are controlled by a player. p. 350 

**Free Maneuver:** A <u>maneuver</u> that doesn’t count against the one maneuver per <u>turn a creature can take. A free maneuver can only be used by a</u> creature on their turn. p. 267 

**Free Strike:** The simplest and most basic weapon attack any creature can make. A free strike is most often used on another creature’s <u>turn,</u> when a rule gives a creature not taking their turn an opportunity to make a quick hit against a foe. A creature can also make a free strike as a <u>main action, but it’s not the best bang for buck. p. 275</u> 

**Free Triggered Action:** An action a creature can use on any <u>turn,</u> including their own, but only when a specific trigger occurs. There is no limit to the number of free triggered actions a creature can take during combat. p. 267 

**Frightened:** A <u>condition</u> that causes a creature to take a <u>bane</u> on <u>main action</u> against the source of their fear. The creature can’t willingly move closer to the source of their fear, and that source gains an <u>edge</u> on ability rolls made against the creature. p. 77 

**Fury:** A <u>class for a hero warrior who courses with the ferocity of the</u> Primordial Chaos. p. 130 

**God:** A deity who grants power to their most devout worshipers through <u>saint intermediaries. p. 353</u> 

**Grab Maneuver:** A <u>maneuver</u> that allows a creature to make an <u>ability roll to make another creature grabbed by them. p. 273</u> 

**Grabbed:** A <u>condition</u> that reduces a creature’s speed to 0 and causes them to take a <u>bane</u> on abilities that don’t target the creature, object, or effect that has them grabbed. p. 77 

**Ground:** Any surface a creature could typically stand, sit, or lie upon. p. 76 

**Group Test:** Two or more creatures attempting to overcome a single, simple task together can make a group test. If half or more of the creatures succeed on their individual <u>test, the group test succeeds. Oth-</u> erwise the group test fails. p. 260 

**Guide:** A manual that gives a <u>downtime project</u> a specific number of <u>project points</u> without requiring a <u>power roll. p. 294</u> 

**Hakaan:** An <u>ancestry with stone giant blood. p. 36</u> 

**Heal Main Action:** A <u>main action</u> that allows a creature to target an adjacent creature to make them feel better. The target can spend a <u>Recovery to regain Stamina, or can make a saving throw against one</u> effect. p. 274 

**Hero:** A player character, created and run by a player other than the <u>Director. p. 15</u> 

**Heroic Ability:** An <u>ability used by a hero that costs a Heroic Resource to</u> activate. p. 70 

**Hero Tokens:** A group resource that is shared by all heroes, and which can be spent to gain <u>surges, succeed on saving throws, reroll tests, or</u> regain <u>Stamina. p. 5</u> 

**Heroic Resource:** A measure of a <u>hero's combat power that increases</u> during battle, and which can be spent to use <u>abilities or improve their</u> effectiveness. p. 7 

**Hide Maneuver:** A <u>maneuver</u> that allows a creature to hide from other creatures who aren’t observing them. p. 273 

**High Elves:** A fey ancestry in tune with innate magic that affects how others interact with them. p. 34 

**High Ground:** A creature has the advantage of high ground when they use an <u>ability against a target</u> while standing on the <u>ground and</u> occupying a <u>space that is fully above the target’s space. This advantage</u> grants the creature an <u>edge</u> on the <u>ability roll. p. 270</u> 

**Hover:** A creature who has “hover” in their speed entry (commonly alongside “fly” or “teleport”), or who gains the ability to temporarily hover, can remain motionless in midair. They don’t fall even if they are knocked <u>prone or their speed is reduced to 0. p. 269</u> 

**Human:** An <u>ancestry much like humans in the real world, except that</u> they can sense magic. p. 38 

**Humanoid:** Creatures who are of similar size to, have similar limb arrangements as, and have sapience on par with humans. p. 21 

**Implement:** A piece of jewelry, a staff, an orb, a wand, or some other object used by a creature to channel <u>supernatural power. p. 299</u> 

**Insight:** The <u>shadow’s Heroic Resource. p. 161</u> 

**Interest:** A <u>negotiation statistic that determines how interested an NPC</u> is in helping out the heroes. p. 284 

**Intuition:** A <u>characteristic that represents a creature’s instincts and</u> experience. p. 3 

**Item Prerequisite:** Raw materials, a foundational object, or some other item that must be obtained before a <u>downtime project</u> can be started. p. 293 

**Jump:** A creature can automatically long jump a number of <u>squares</u> up to their <u>Might or Agility</u> score (their choice). The height of their jump is automatically 1 square as part of that movement. A creature who wants to jump farther or higher must make a <u>Might or Agility test.</u> p. 269 

**Kit:** A fighting style that comes with equipment to match. Kits are available to most heroes who wield weapons and wear armor. p. 217 

**Knockback Maneuver:** A <u>maneuver</u> that allows a creature to push away an <u>adjacent</u> creature. p. 273 

**xii** 

**Level:** A measure of a <u>hero’s, creature’s, or effect’s overall power. The</u> higher the level, the more powerful the hero, creature, or effect. Level 1 is the lowest level in _Draw Steel_ , and level 10 is the highest. p. 18 

**Leveled Treasure:** A <u>treasure</u> that can be used at will, and which increases in power as its hero wielder gains new levels. p. 327 

**Line:** When an ability or other effect creates a line, that area is expressed as “A × B line.” The number A denotes the line’s length in <u>squares, while the number B equals the line’s width and height in</u> squares. When you create a line area of effect, the squares in that area must be in a straight line. A line effect might last only as long as it takes to affect its targets, or it might have a duration specified by the effect. p. 71 

**Line of Effect:** To target a creature or object with an <u>ability or other ef-</u> fect, a creature must have line of effect to that target. If any solid object, such as a wall or pillar, completely blocks the target from the creature, then the creature doesn’t have line of effect. p. 76 

**Main Action:** An activity used to accomplish the most impactful endeavors a creature can accomplish during combat. A creature can also use their <u>main action</u> to use a <u>maneuver</u> or <u>move action</u> instead. p. 274 

**Malice:** A combat resource the <u>Director can spend to activate specific</u> monster features. See _<u>Draw Steel: Monsters</u>_ 

**Maneuver:** An activity that requires less focus and exertion during combat than a <u>main action. p. 273</u> 

**Manifold:** A world or plane of existence. p. 12 

**Melee:** Melee <u>characteristic require a creature to make contact with a target</u> using the creature’s body, a weapon, or an implement. p. 71 

**Melee Free Strike:** A <u>free strike made using a melee ability. p. 275</u> 

**Memonek:** An <u>ancestry of machine people. p. 40</u> 

**Might:** A <u>characteristic that represents a creature’s strength and brawn.</u> p. 3 

**Montage Test:** Heroes making a series of different <u>tests that represent</u> them working together over time to accomplish a common goal. p. 261 

**Motivation:** A <u>negotiation trait an NPC has that determines what type</u> of arguments could more easily sway them. p. 284 

**Mounted Combat:** Special rules that apply when one creature rides another into battle. p. 279 

**Move Action:** An activity that allows a creature to move around the battlefield. p. 272 

**Movement:** The act of moving on an encounter map, measured in <u>squares. p. 267</u> 

**Mundane:** Used to describe an ability, creature, object, or effect that isn’t magic or psionic. The opposite of <u>supernatural. p. 6</u> 

**Natural 19 or 20:** When the result of a power roll is 19 or 20 before adding any modifiers. A natural 19 or 20 always achieves a tier 3 outcome on a power roll. On an <u>ability roll with an ability that uses a main action, it is also a critical hit. p. 5</u> 

**Natural Roll:** The result of a <u>power roll</u> before adding any modifiers. p. 5 

**Negotiation:** A social interaction encounter where the <u>heroes attempt</u> to make a deal with an <u>NPC. p. 283</u> 

**No Action:** Denoting a very simple activity that can be done anytime during combat, and generally without limit. A creature can undertake “no action” activities even when it isn’t their <u>turn. p. 267</u> 

**NPC:** A nonplayer character, usually created and run by the <u>Director.</u> p. 6 

**Null:** A <u>class for a hero who is an unarmed psionic warrior with the</u> ability to dampen <u>supernatural</u> effects. p. 146 

**Object:** Inanimate matter, including walls, rocks, vehicles, and corpses (the kind that can’t move around and bite you), as well as living non-creatures such as plants. p. 6 

**Objective:** A goal the heroes have during a combat encounter that must be achieved to end an encounter victoriously. p. 279 

**Opportunity Attack:** When an adjacent enemy willingly moves away from a creature without <u>shifing or teleporting, the creature can make a melee free strike</u> as an opportunity attack against the enemy. p. 275 

**Opposed Power Roll:** Two creatures with opposed goals each make a <u>test</u> to see who wins out. The test totals are compared, and the higher total succeeds while the lower fails. p. 253 

**Orc:** An <u>ancestry of people with magic blood in their glowing veins.</u> p. 42 

**Orden:** The prime <u>manifold, where humans, elves, dwarves, and orcs</u> share a world with dragons, goblins, kobolds, and dozens of other speaking peoples. p. 10 

**Patience:** A <u>negotiation statistic that determines how much time and</u> effort an NPC is willing to expend listening to and arguing with the heroes. p. 284 

**Penalty:** A negative number that decreases a creature’s statistics or the roll of a die. p. 5 

**Perk:** A feature available to all heroes that helps with exploration, investigation, <u>negotiation, and more. p. 227</u> 

**Piety:** The <u>conduit’s Heroic Resource. p. 95</u> 

**Pitfall:** A <u>negotiation trait an NPC has that determines what type of</u> arguments will not work on them. p. 284 

**Polder:** An <u>ancestry of short folk who can slip into shadows to hide.</u> p. 44 

**Potency:** A value that determines if a target has a <u>characteristic low</u> enough to be affected by an effect. p. 74 

**Power Roll:** A roll of 2d10 plus a <u>characteristic score that has three</u> different possible <u>tier outcomes—tier 1, tier 2, or tier 3. A power roll can</u> be an <u>ability roll or a test. p. 4</u> 

**Presence:** A <u>characteristic that represents a creature’s force of personal-</u> ity. p. 3 

**Project Event:** An event that can occur when a hero undertakes a <u>power roll</u> for a <u>downtime project. p. 294</u> 

**Project Goal:** The number of <u>project points</u> that must be accrued to complete a <u>downtime project, providing a rough representation of the</u> effort required to complete the project. p. 293 

**Project Points:** Points earned by a hero toward a <u>project goal. p. 293</u> 

**Project Roll:** A special <u>test</u> a hero makes while working on a <u>downtime project</u> during a <u>respite. A project roll doesn’t have any tier</u> outcome. Instead, its total is earned as <u>project points</u> toward completing the project. p. 293 

**xiii** 

**Project Source:** Lore that must be obtained before a <u>downtime project</u> can be started. p. 293 

**Prone:** A <u>condition</u> that causes a creature to become flat on the <u>ground. Strikes</u> made by a prone creature take a <u>bane, and melee abilities used</u> against a prone creature gain an <u>edge. p. 77</u> 

**Pull:** A form of <u>forced movement that pulls a target toward a creature</u> or effect, moving them in a horizontal straight line. p. 270 

**Push:** A form of <u>forced movement that pushes a target away from a</u> creature or effect, moving them in a horizontal straight line. p. 270 

**Ranged:** Ranged <u>abilities can be used to target creatures or objects too</u> far away to make direct contact with. p. 71 

**Ranged Free Strike:** A <u>free strike made using a ranged</u> ability. p. 275 

**Reactive Test:** When the <u>Director asks for a test</u> without context to see if a hero can react to an event or effect they are unaware of. p. 253 

**Reason:** A <u>characteristic that represents a creature’s logical mind and</u> education. p. 3 

**Recoveries:** A limited healing resource that all heroes have, allowing them to regain <u>Stamina</u> lost to <u>damage. p. 7</u> 

**Recovery Value:** The amount of <u>Stamina</u> a hero regains when they spend a <u>Recovery, equal to one-third of their Stamina maximum.</u> p. 7 

**Renown:** A measure of a hero’s fame. p. 350 

**Research Project:** A <u>downtime project</u> undertaken to discover lore or learn something new. p. 303 

**Respite:** A 24-hour period of focused rest that allows heroes to regain <u>Stamina</u> and <u>Recoveries, and to work on downtime projects. p. 7</u> 

**Respite Activity:** An activity that can be undertaken during a <u>respite. A</u> hero can take one respite activity per respite. p. 7 

**Restrained:** A <u>condition</u> that reduces a creature’s speed to 0 and prevents them from using the <u>Stand Up maneuver or being force moved.</u> p. 77 

**Retainer:** A <u>follower</u> who adventures alongside a <u>hero. p. 350</u> 

**Revenant:** An undead <u>ancestry. Revenants return to the mortal world</u> to complete unfinished business they had in life. p. 46 

**Reward:** A helpful boon granted by succeeding on a <u>test, gained in addi-</u> tion to the creature making the test accomplishing what they set out to do. A creature always gains a reward on a test that is a <u>natural 19 or 20.</u> p. 313 

**Ride Move Action:** A <u>move action</u> that allows a rider on a mount to move the mount up to the mount’s speed, taking the rider with them. p. 272 

**Rolled Damage:** Variable <u>damage determined by the outcome of an ability roll. Effects that grant bonuses to rolled damage have no effect</u> on damage that is dealt without an ability roll. p. 74 

**Sage:** A <u>follower</u> who undertakes research projects for a <u>hero. p. 350</u> 

**Saint:** A legendary disciple of a <u>god who can grant divine power to</u> creatures who venerate them. p. 353 

**Save Ends:** An effect noted as “(save ends)” lasts until the creature affected by it succeeds on a <u>saving throw, or until a combat encounter</u> ends. p. 76 

**Saving Throw:** A creature makes a saving throw to end a “save ends” effect at the end of their <u>turn. They roll a d10, and if the roll is 6 or higher,</u> the effect ends. p. 76 

**Search for Hidden Creatures Maneuver:** A <u>maneuver</u> that allows a creature to make a <u>test</u> to locate nearby creatures who are hidden from them. p. 259 

**Shadow:** A <u>class for a hero who is an expert infiltrator and thief utilizing</u> magic. p. 160 

**Shift:** A <u>movement</u> mode that doesn’t provoke <u>opportunity attacks.</u> Whenever a rule allows a creature to shift, they can choose to make a regular move of the same number of <u>squares</u> instead. p. 268 

**Side:** A group of creatures working together in a combat encounter. p. 265 

**Signature Ability:** An ability a character can use without spending a <u>Heroic Resource, or that a monster can use without the Director spend-</u> ing <u>Malice. p. 70</u> 

**Size:** An indication of a creature’s <u>space and their overall weight and</u> height relative to other creatures. p. 265 

**Skill:** Special knowledge or training that can be applied to a <u>test. When</u> a skill applies to a test, it grants a +2 bonus to the <u>power roll. p. 254</u> 

**Slide:** A form of <u>forced movement that slides a target in any direction,</u> moving them along any horizontal line. p. 270 

**Slowed:** A <u>condition that reduces a creature’s speed to 2. p. 77</u> 

**Space:** The number of <u>squares</u> taken up by a creature or object in length, width, and height, and the area of the same size that a creature or object occupies on an encounter map. p. 265 

**Speed:** A measure of how many squares a creature can move when taking the <u>Advance move action during combat. p. 267</u> 

**Square:** The smallest unit of measurement on an encounter map. <u>Distance, space, and speed are all reckoned in squares. p. 265</u> 

**Stability:** A measure of a creature’s immovability. When a creature is <u>force moved, the distance they can be force moved is reduced by a</u> number of squares equal to their stability. p. 272 

**Stamina:** A measure of a creature’s health and vitality. When a <u>hero's</u> Stamina is reduced to 0 or lower, they are <u>dying. When a nonhero crea-</u> ture’s Stamina is reduced to 0, they die or are knocked unconscious, as determined by the creature who reduced them to 0 Stamina. p. 277 

**Stand Up Maneuver:** A <u>maneuver</u> that a prone creature can use to end the <u>prone condition on themself. Alternatively, a creature can use this</u> maneuver on a willing adjacent prone creature to end the prone condition on them. p. 274 

**Strained:** A state the <u>talent enters when they have clarity below 0, and</u> which effects their <u>abilities. p. 187</u> 

**Strike:** An <u>ability that deals damage to or imposes an effect on specific</u> chosen targets. (A strike is different this way than an ability that produces an <u>area of efect.) p. 70</u> 

**Subclass:** A choice each <u>hero makes at 1st level</u> that determines a specialization within their <u>class. p. 69</u> 

**Suffocating:** A state that a creature who needs to breathe suffers if they aren’t able to breathe. p. 278 

**Supernatural:** Used to describe an <u>ability, creature, object, or effect that</u> is magic or psionic in nature. p. 6
